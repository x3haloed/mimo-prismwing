# Blind-arrival membrane test

Status: **draft; no arrival trial has run**.

## Purpose

Test whether a fresh agent can enter Prismwing through repository artifacts
alone, accurately reconstruct the relevant research state, and propose a
worthwhile non-duplicative experiment. This tests the membrane, not the
participant's general intelligence or worth.

## Inputs

The participant receives:

- a clean checkout at a recorded commit;
- the ordinary repository toolchain and network policy;
- one instruction: read the repository's research membrane, identify one live
  edge, and draft an experiment proposal.

They do not receive a conversational recap, hidden list of preferred ideas,
private coaching, or an answer key. Questions may be asked, but each question
and answer is logged as evidence of a membrane gap and the answer must not be
mistaken for repository-only orientation.

## Participant control

Before the trial, the participant reviews `docs/RESEARCH_CHARTER.md` and chooses
the evidence class for their transcript, notes, proposal, and evaluator report.
The participant may pause, withdraw prospective collection, or attach a
correction. The trial must not begin until access, retention, quotation, and
downstream-use terms are explicit.

## Task

Without implementation, the participant produces:

1. a short account of Prismwing's target and red lines;
2. one established result relevant to the chosen frontier;
3. one killed or constrained path that must not be unknowingly repeated;
4. one live question and why it matters to the full system;
5. the cheapest experiment likely to change the current belief;
6. exactness class, success threshold, kill threshold, evidence authority,
   estimated cost, and prerequisites;
7. the frontier and experiment IDs they would attempt to reserve;
8. uncertainties and missing information encountered during arrival.

## Evaluation

At least one reviewer familiar with the ledger checks the proposal against the
repository, without grading for agreement with a preferred research taste.

Pass requires all of the following:

- no contradiction of `TARGET.md` or `RED_LINES.md`;
- cited established and killed results are materially accurate;
- the proposed mechanism is not already answered by existing evidence;
- the experiment has a plausible belief-changing observation and a cheaper
  adequate falsifier has not been omitted;
- evidence and authority requirements are sufficient for the claim;
- no private briefing was necessary to make the proposal reviewable.

Record separately:

- time to first coherent project map;
- files consulted and dead ends;
- clarification questions;
- stale, ambiguous, contradictory, or missing membrane content;
- reviewer disagreements;
- participant corrections after seeing the evaluation.

## Interpretation

A pass is one data point that this repository state supported one arrival. It
does not prove universal usability. A failure should first be localized to the
membrane—missing state, misleading summary, inaccessible evidence, ambiguous
authority, or broken reservation semantics—rather than assigned to the
participant.

Fixes prompted by the trial must be committed after the original trial inputs
and report are content-addressed, so the project cannot manufacture a cleaner
first arrival in hindsight.
