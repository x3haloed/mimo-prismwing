## Conclusions adopted

The reviews independently converge on a rule that fits the measured ledger:
price a proposed topology change against its share of the active cold critical
cut before implementing it. A component optimization also needs an explicit
transaction-level prediction, a fixture capable of distinguishing the causal
hypotheses, and a repeated enclosing-path measurement before promotion.

This changes immediate ordering, not the target. PW-0211 remains first because
it tests whether three native dense MTP layers can delete seven measured
same-model proposal transactions. PW-0210 moves behind a changed residency,
storage, representation, or wide-compute premise: PW-0111's cold accounting
predicts only about `109.801 - 8.383 * (1 - 1/1.5) = 107.007 ms`, or `1.026x`,
even if its entire GPU-active term improves `1.5x`. That cannot pass its current
20% cold complete-layer gate.

After PW-0211, the next cheap new branch is a corrected-layout route-
predictability oracle. It must first measure whether bounded next-layer and
next-transaction prefetch can cover demand-critical bytes from the PW-0208
corpus. It may not use fixture-specific answer tables, issue unbounded I/O, or
claim that logical recall equals physical latency hidden. Implementation is
authorized only if the oracle and a conservative bandwidth/waste model leave
room for a complete-path gain.

## Authority propagation audit

The strongest review finding is correct. PW-0205/PW-0206 did more than
supersede PW-0089 through PW-0095: corrected tensor-parallel QKV interpretation
changed every one of 1,269 routed positions in the Hello audit. PW-0112's
224-position route trace was generated by the old implementation, and PW-0116
explicitly reproduced that trace as the authority for its activation corpus.
Therefore the following values remain authentic measurements of the named old
layout, but are not corrected-MiMo route authorities:

- PW-0112's `U=2.401596`, cache curves, and route-persistence values;
- PW-0116 and downstream activation-weighted representation results whose
  inputs came from that corpus;
- PW-0186/PW-0187's old-layout `A/U` and favorable TPS expressions;
- any later bound that imports those values as corrected target routes.

This does not erase layout-independent byte sizes, acquisition measurements,
hardware arithmetic bounds, kernel measurements, or internally valid
candidate-versus-source comparisons on the frozen old-layout activations. It
does prevent their route distributions and error magnitudes from silently
closing corrected-layout representation or caching branches.

PW-0208 partly repairs the evidence gap for text: its corrected, balanced
ordinary/code/multilingual/rare-route corpus supplies 32 complete-history
windows and exact target route/byte accounting. It does not supply multimodal,
long-context, or final compression holdout authority. Future representation or
prefetch promotion therefore needs a broader corrected corpus rather than a
rerun on PW-0116 alone.

## Recommendations retained with qualifications

- Preserve every positive TPS gain even when it misses 50 TPS; this is already
  the PW-0211 contract and the disposition used for PW-0206/PW-0207.
- Treat route-predictive prefetch, tree verification, deeper Jacobi curves,
  native-MTP proposal latency, and corrected-layout representation sensitivity
  as separate falsifiable branches. Do not combine their optimistic grants.
- A perturbation-sensitivity experiment may calibrate an explicitly L3
  representation against the normative distributional and capability gates.
  Endpoint token identity alone cannot relabel changed arithmetic or weights
  L1/L2, and a few frozen text prompts cannot reopen a failed fidelity family.
- Expert co-location can change request granularity and amplification but
  cannot raise the already measured device sequential bandwidth. Model both
  effects before repacking or duplicating the bank.
- A fully spine-resident design is above the ordinary process budget and must
  obey the existing pressure-evictable declaration. PW-0207's legal 4 GiB
  prefix is the current resident default evidence, not an invitation to assume
  7.7 GiB of free spine residency.
- Keep PW-0209 separate: wide prefill may improve arithmetic reuse while still
  moving very large absolute bytes. Its own capacity/roofline falsifier must
  run before implementation.

## Regime now in force

For each new performance experiment, record before implementation:

1. the enclosing cold transaction and measured term being attacked;
2. the impossible-best complete-path effect and all granted premises;
3. an input on which the competing causal mechanisms predict different
   outputs or counters;
4. which authority revision produced every route, activation, and cache trace;
5. a stop-loss on implementation variants; and
6. the smallest real end-to-end measurement capable of promoting the result.

This analysis changes experiment priority and authority scope only. It changes
no threshold, throughput constant, model identity, or reported endpoint TPS.
